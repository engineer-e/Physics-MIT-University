Mental Model: A slow-moving marble falling in a heavy fluid (like honey or oil) experiences a resistance that grows linearly with its speed. This opposes gravity, pushing upward.

5.2 FLUID DYNAMICS TAKEAWAYS & CRITICAL REASONING
  • Geometric independence: The drag coefficient α depends purely on physical dimensions, shape, and fluid viscosity—not the object's mass density.
  • Dynamic Equilibrium: Terminal speed is reached because drag increases with velocity. Thus, the falling body self-regulates its acceleration to zero.
  • Terminal limit limit: No matter how high you drop an object from in a viscous fluid, its falling speed will mathematically asymptotically cap at m·g / α.
