■ Continuous Mathematical Derivation

To find the tension $T(x)$ at an arbitrary distance $x$ measured downwards from the ceiling, we establish a coordinate system where $x \in [0, L]$.

1. Macroscopic Equilibrium Method

For a segment cut at point $x$, the remaining portion below has length $L - x$. Assuming a uniform mass density $\lambda = M/L$:

$$M_{\text{below}}(x) = \frac{L - x}{L} M$$

For the isolated portion to remain stationary, upward tension $T(x)$ must balance gravity acting on $M_{\text{below}}$:

$$T(x) = M_{\text{below}} g = M g \left(1 - \frac{x}{L}\right)$$

2. Differential Analysis Method

Consider an infinitesimal slice of rope of thickness $dx$ situated at position $x$. We analyze the three forces acting upon it to maintain equilibrium:

  • An upward tension $T(x)$ exerted by the rope above it.
  • A downward tension $T(x + dx)$ pulling from below.
  • A downward gravity force acting on its mass element $dm = \lambda(x) dx$.

Applying Newton's Second Law ($\Sigma F_x = 0$ in the downward direction):

$$T(x + dx) + dm \cdot g - T(x) = 0$$

Rearranging and substituting $dm = \lambda(x) dx$:

$$\frac{T(x + dx) - T(x)}{dx} = -\lambda(x) g$$

Taking the mathematical limit as $dx \to 0$ yields the continuous ordinary differential equation (ODE):

$$\frac{dT}{dx} = -\lambda(x) g$$

This dynamic differential system is integrated from position $x$ to the bottom boundary of the rope at $x=L$, where the boundary condition dictates tension must vanish ($T(L) = 0$):

$$\int_{T(x)}^{T(L)} dT' = \int_{x}^{L} -\lambda(x') g \, dx'$$

$$T(x) = g \int_{x}^{L} \lambda(x') \, dx'$$

■ Discretization & Numerical Integration

How does our computer program calculate tension in real time, especially for non-uniform clumpy distributions? It translates the continuous calculus differential equation into a discrete numerical sum.

The Computational Mesh

We divide the rope of length $L$ into $N$ equal discrete steps of size:

$$\Delta x = \frac{L}{N}$$

This turns the continuous position $x$ into a series of $N+1$ discrete grid coordinates: $x_i = i \cdot \Delta x$ for $i \in [0, N]$.

Backward Euler Summation Algorithm

Our boundary state begins at the bottom free end $i = N$ where $T[N] = 0$.

To solve the differential equation backward up to the ceiling, we rewrite $\frac{dT}{dx} = -\lambda(x)g$ as a difference equation:

$$\frac{T[i+1] - T[i]}{\Delta x} = -\lambda(x_i) g$$

Solving for $T[i]$, we obtain our stepping recurrence relation:

$$T[i] = T[i+1] + \lambda(x_i) \cdot g \cdot \Delta x$$

Solver Loop Execution (JavaScript)

// 1. Initialize boundary condition at bottom
let T = new Array(N + 1);
T[N] = 0.0;
let dx = L / N;

// 2. Loop backwards from bottom up to ceiling
for (let i = N - 1; i >= 0; i--) {
    let x_i = i * dx;
    let localDensity = getDensity(x_i);
    let dm = localDensity * dx;
    
    // Euler Step
    T[i] = T[i + 1] + dm * g;
}
