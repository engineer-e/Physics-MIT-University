Professor's Mental Model

When writing Newton's Second Law for rotating systems, we treat the equation as a bridge between two fundamentally different concepts:

Left Side: Dynamics (Why) This side contains the forces that physical things exert (friction, gravity, tension, normal force). You draw a Free-Body Diagram to find them.
Right Side: Kinematics (How) This is the geometry of the circular path itself. It tells us how much acceleration is required to keep moving in that circle.
Crucial Insight: For any true circular path, the radial force can never be zero. If the sum of Fr becomes zero, the object instantly flies off along a straight-line tangent, obeying Newton's first law!
