The Dynamics of Motion

Connecting kinematics to forces through Newton's Second Law.

1. Visual Infographic

The Paradigm Shift

Aristotle (Ancient Mechanics)

Believed an applied force is required to maintain a body in uniform motion. (An illusion caused by friction).

Isaac Newton

Realized applied forces cause changes in an object's motion (acceleration), rather than being necessary to maintain it.

2. Formulas & Algorithms

Core Formulas

  • Newton's 2nd Law
    $$\Sigma \vec{F} = m \cdot \vec{a}$$
  • Gravity (Weight)
    $$\vec{F}_g = m \cdot \vec{g}$$
  • Friction
    $$f \le \mu \cdot F_N$$
  • Hooke's Law (Spring)
    $$\vec{F}_s = -k \cdot \vec{x}$$

Step-by-Step Solution Algorithm

  1. Identify the object of interest.
  2. Draw a Free Body Diagram (FBD) showing all forces.
  3. Choose a convenient coordinate system (e.g., $x$-axis parallel to motion).
  4. Resolve forces into $x$ and $y$ components.
  5. Apply Newton's Second Law to each axis:
    $$\Sigma F_x = m \cdot a_x$$ $$\Sigma F_y = m \cdot a_y$$
  6. Solve the resulting equations for unknowns.

Computation Algorithm (Simulator Loop)

  1. Calculate Normal Force:
    $$F_N = m \cdot g$$
  2. Determine max static friction:
    $$f_{\text{max}} = \mu \cdot F_N$$
  3. Calculate Net Force ($F_{\text{net}}$) based on Applied Force ($F_{\text{app}}$) and velocity ($v$).
    • If moving ($v \ne 0$):
      $$F_{\text{net}} = F_{\text{app}} - \text{sign}(v) \cdot f_{\text{max}}$$
    • If stationary & $|F_{\text{app}}| > f_{\text{max}}$:
      $$F_{\text{net}} = F_{\text{app}} - \text{sign}(F_{\text{app}}) \cdot f_{\text{max}}$$
    • Else (static):
      $$F_{\text{net}} = 0$$
  4. Update Acceleration:
    $$a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m}$$
  5. Numerical Integration (Euler Method):
    • Velocity update:
      $$v_{\text{new}} = v_{\text{old}} + a \cdot \Delta t$$
    • Position update:
      $$x_{\text{new}} = x_{\text{old}} + v_{\text{new}} \cdot \Delta t$$
