Mathematical Solver Block

1. Sliding Downward INACTIVE

Friction acts *upward* to oppose descent. Acceleration is positive down the plane.

2. Compressing Spring INACTIVE

Spring force acts *upwards* proportional to compression ($x$). Force of friction flips with motion.

3. Sliding Upward INACTIVE

Friction flips, acting *downward* along with gravity, resulting in rapid deceleration.

4. Static Equilibrium INACTIVE

The block is at rest. Static friction exactly balances gravity.

Numerical Integration & Step Algorithm

This simulator models non-linear discontinuous forces using the high-accuracy Verlet Euler-Cromer Integrator. Unlike plain Euler integration, it naturally conserves energy in periodic cycles by calculating the upcoming step velocity *prior* to displacement calculation.

1. Acceleration update $a_{t} = \Sigma F(s_{t}, v_{t}) / m$
2. Symplectic state shift $v_{t+dt} = v_t + a_t \cdot dt$ $s_{t+dt} = s_t + v_{t+dt} \cdot dt$
