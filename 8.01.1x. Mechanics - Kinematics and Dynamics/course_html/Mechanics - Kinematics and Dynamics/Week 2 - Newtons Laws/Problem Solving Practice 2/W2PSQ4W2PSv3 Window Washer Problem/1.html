Window Washer Problem Explained

In standard textbook problems, a window washer of mass \(m_w\) stands on a platform of mass \(m_p\), holding a rope that loops over a pulley setup to suspend them.

The Golden Systems Matrix:

  • System 1 (Washer): Experiences gravity (\(m_w g\)), upward normal force from the platform (\(N\)), and the pulling tension from the rope (\(T_1\)) pulling their hands up.
  • System 2 (Platform): Experiences gravity (\(m_p g\)), downward contact normal force from the washer (\(N\)), and the upward tension of String 2 (\(T_2\)).
  • System 3 (Combined): Here, the contact normal force \(N\) becomes an internal force and cancels out, leaving only external weights and tensions.
  • System 4 (Full Composite): Including Pulley A means String 2's tension \(T_2\) also becomes internal! We are left with simple upward force of \(3T_1\) and total weight \((m_w+m_p)g\).
