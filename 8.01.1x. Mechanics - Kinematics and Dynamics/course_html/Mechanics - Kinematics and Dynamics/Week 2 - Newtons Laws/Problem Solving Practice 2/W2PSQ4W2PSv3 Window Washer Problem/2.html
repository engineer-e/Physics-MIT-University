Concept Study The Power of System Boundaries

Isolating each individual piece of this setup requires solving for interaction forces between the person and platform, and the tension of String 2.

Instead, we draw a system boundary around the Platform + Washerperson + Pulley A. Since intermediate internal interactions cancel out by Newton's Third Law, we can analyze the setup with one single elegant equation.
