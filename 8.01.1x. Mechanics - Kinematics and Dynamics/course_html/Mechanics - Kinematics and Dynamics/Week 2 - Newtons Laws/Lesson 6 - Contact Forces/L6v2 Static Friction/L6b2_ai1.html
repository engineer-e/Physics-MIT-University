Mathematical & Computational Flow

1 Normal & Gravitational Equilibrium

Before calculating friction constraints, compute vertical force balance: \(N = m \cdot g = \text{mass} \times 9.81\text{ m/s}^2\)

2 Friction Boundary Thresholds

Compute the threshold bounds for transitions: \(f_{s,\max} = \mu_s N\) \(f_k = \mu_k N\)

3a Regime: Static Resistance (\(v = 0\) and \(|F| \le f_{s,\max}\))

Applied force does not exceed limits. Force is perfectly balanced by static resistance: \(f_s = -F_{\text{app}} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad a = 0\)

3b Regime: Kinetic Slip (\(|F| > f_{s,\max}\) or \(v \neq 0\))

Applied force breaks static welds. Interlocking asperities shear; friction drops instantly to kinetic resistance: \(f = -\operatorname{sgn}(v) f_k\) \(a = \frac{F_{\text{app}} + f}{m} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad v_{t} = v_0 + a \cdot \Delta t\)
