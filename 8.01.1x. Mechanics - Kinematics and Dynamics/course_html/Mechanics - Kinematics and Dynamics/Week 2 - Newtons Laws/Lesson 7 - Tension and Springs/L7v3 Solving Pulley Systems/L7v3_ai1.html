Step 1 Frictionless Check

State Physical Assumptions

Assume rope and pulley are completely frictionless. The pulley remains at static rest ($P = 0$), so we focus strictly on the dynamics of $m_1$ and $m_2$.

Step 2 Action-Reaction Pairs

Identify Active Force Fields

The tension force vector acting upward from the masses to the rope forms an action-reaction pair matching Newton's Third Law.

$T_{r,1} = T_{1,r} \quad \text{and} \quad T_{r,2} = T_{2,r} = T$
Step 3 Unit Vectors

Assign Coordinate Systems

We assign individual unit vectors for each block. To keep acceleration scalars positive when $m_1 > m_2$:

For $m_1$: $\hat{j}_1$ is downwards.
For $m_2$: $\hat{j}_2$ is upwards.
Step 4 Newton's 2nd Law

Setup Dynamical Equations

Applying $\sum F = m a$ along each custom coordinate vector yields:

Block 1: $m_1 g - T = m_1 a$
Block 2: $T - m_2 g = m_2 a$
Step 5 Algebraic Matrix

Solve Dynamically

By combining both equations to eliminate the Tension scalar ($T$), we solve directly for acceleration ($a$):

$$a = \frac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2} g$$ $$T = \frac{2 m_1 m_2}{m_1 + m_2} g$$
