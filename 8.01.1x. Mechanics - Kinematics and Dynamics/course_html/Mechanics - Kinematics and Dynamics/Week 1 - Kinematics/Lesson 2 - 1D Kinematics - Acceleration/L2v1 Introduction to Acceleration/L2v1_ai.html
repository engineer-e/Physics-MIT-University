🧠 Perception vs. Reality Check

How our eyes view it

Our retinas register motion displacements. Because the object stops moving momentarily at its peak, our mind tricks us into believing acceleration pauses alongside velocity.

Is Acceleration Constant or Polynomial?

It is a flat, continuous constant. Near Earth's surface, the acceleration doesn't vary with time or altitude; it remains a steady vector pointing straight down.
