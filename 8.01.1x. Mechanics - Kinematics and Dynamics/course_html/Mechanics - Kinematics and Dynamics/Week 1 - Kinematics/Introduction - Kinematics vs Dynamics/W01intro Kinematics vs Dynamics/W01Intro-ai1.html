How vs. Why: The Great Physics Divide

Classical mechanics asks two entirely distinct questions. Newton's Second Law serves as the profound intellectual bridge connecting them.

Left-Hand Side (LHS) The Cause

Dynamics

Focuses on Forces ($\Sigma F$). It details the physical interactions (gravity, friction, tension) pushing or pulling an object to alter its state of rest or motion.

Right-Hand Side (RHS) The Effect

Kinematics

Focuses on the **Geometry of Motion** ($m \cdot a$). It describes the trajectory, speed, position, and acceleration over time without asking what caused them.

Geometry & Graph Panel

Dynamics (The Catalyst)

  • • Fundamental Question: What forces are acting? Why is motion changing?
  • • Primary Entities: Gravitational Attraction, Tension Force, Surface Friction, Active Propulsion thrusts ($F_{\text{net}}$).

Kinematics (The Description)

  • • Fundamental Question: Where is the object? How fast is it moving? Which path does it take?
  • • Primary Entities: Position ($x$), Velocity ($v$), Rate of Speed Change ($a$), Visual Trajectory pathing.
