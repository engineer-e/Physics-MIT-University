Calculus of Motion Infographic

Position Vector

The location of a runner moving forward on a 1D track relative to origin & unit direction vector \(\hat{i}\):

\(\vec{x}(t) = x(t) \hat{i}\)
Average Velocity Component

The slope of the secant connecting two finite points across interval \(\Delta t\):

\(\vec{v}_{\text{ave}} = \frac{x(t + \Delta t) - x(t)}{\Delta t} \hat{i} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} \hat{i}\)
The Limit Process (\(\Delta t \to 0\))

Shrinking \(\Delta t\) creates a sequence of values converging on the instantaneous tangent slope:

\(\vec{v}(T_1) = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{x(T_1 + \Delta t) - x(T_1)}{\Delta t} \hat{i} = \frac{dx}{dt} \hat{i}\)

Key takeaway: An average velocity measures net spatial displacement across a span of time. An instantaneous velocity is a single-moment derivative, calculated from the infinite limit of average velocities.
