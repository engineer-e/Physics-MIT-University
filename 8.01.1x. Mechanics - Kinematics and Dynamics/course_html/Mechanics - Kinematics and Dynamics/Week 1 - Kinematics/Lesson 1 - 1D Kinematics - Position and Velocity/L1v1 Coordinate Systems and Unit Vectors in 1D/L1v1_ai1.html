Definitions Reference

  • Origin ($O$): A chosen point where $x = 0$. Absolute freedom of choice!
  • Unit Vector ($\hat{i}$): A helper vector of magnitude exactly 1 pointing in the positive $+x$ axis direction at every physical space.
  • Position Vector ($\vec{r}$): Points from origin directly to runner ($\vec{r} = x\hat{i}$). Represents offset and direction relative to $O$.
